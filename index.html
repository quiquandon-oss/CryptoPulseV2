BTC · Next 24h

A k-NN historical analog model: today's sentiment/technical/regime/bottom-score readout is compared (z-score normalized) against every complete day of history, the most similar days are found, and this is what BTC actually did over the prediction horizon after each of them. The range is the 25th-75th percentile of those outcomes — read it as a spread of what similar setups did, not a guarantee.

Prediction Recap ▼

Every prediction targets a fixed horizon (12h or 24h, whichever tab is selected). "Predicted" is the model's called direction/confidence and expected median move; "Reality" is what actually happened once the horizon passed; "Deviation" is the gap between the expected move and the real one — a small deviation means the model's magnitude sense was good even if the direction call is what gets graded for accuracy. Pending rows haven't reached their horizon mark yet. Scroll within the table to see older predictions — nothing is dropped.

Calibration Over Time

Each point is the Brier score computed using every resolved prediction up to and including it — not a per-prediction score, a running average. Lower is better; the dashed line is the naive "always guess 50/50" baseline (0.25) — a model not beating that has no demonstrated edge. Watch for stability, not a single favorable reading: an early lead that fades as the line matures is the exact trap that made V1's Track Record tile misleading.
