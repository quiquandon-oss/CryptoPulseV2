BTC · Next 24h

A k-NN historical analog model: today's sentiment/technical/regime/bottom-score readout is compared (z-score normalized) against every complete day of history, the most similar days are found, and this is what BTC actually did in the 24h after each of them. The range is the 25th-75th percentile of those outcomes — read it as a spread of what similar setups did, not a guarantee.
